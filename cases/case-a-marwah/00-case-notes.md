## Summary

Dr. Marwah works on computer vision for early detection of crop disease from
multispectral drone and satellite imagery. She completed her PhD at the Ardhana
Institute of Technology (Pune) in 2019, did a postdoc at Coastland State
University, and joined Verdant Systems in 2022, where she leads the crop-health
modeling group.

She is the primary author of the *Lyra* segmentation method, which as far as I
can tell is genuinely well regarded in her subfield. Two agricultural agencies
have adopted it operationally.
