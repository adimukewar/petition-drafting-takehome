Dear Dr. Marwah,

On the recommendation of the associate editors, I am writing to invite you to

articles the journal has published in the last five years, and several associate
editors specifically requested your addition to the board.

Please confirm by 20 February.

With regards,
Dr. Marguerite Vasseur-Lindholm
