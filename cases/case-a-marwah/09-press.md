## Item 2 — Western Grower Technology Review

**Publication:** *Western Grower Technology Review*
**Type:** Independent trade publication, monthly, paid subscription
**Circulation:** ~18,000 (growers, agronomists, agency staff)
**Issue:** March 2025
**Headline:** "The Detection Gap: How Two Agencies Cut Inspection Lag by Two Weeks"

> When the Cascadia Regional Agricultural Board went looking for a way to triage
> its drone imagery, the tool it settled on came out of a university lab rather
> than a vendor booth.
>
> The method, called Lyra, was developed by Ishani Marwah, now a senior
> research scientist at Verdant Systems. Its distinguishing feature is
> tolerance for sparse labeling — a practical concession to the reality that
> nobody can afford to have a pathologist annotate a season of imagery.
>
> "It was the only one that worked on our data without an annotation contract
> attached," said Cascadia chief scientist Halvard Sunde.
>
> Sonoran Valley Water and Crop Authority adopted the approach the following
> season for tree-nut acreage, a materially different imaging problem…

*(Full text 1,400 words. Bylined staff reporter. Marwah interviewed; two
independent sources quoted.)*
