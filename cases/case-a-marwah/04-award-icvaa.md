## Case coordinator note

Dr. Marwah's CV describes this as an "internationally recognized" award. The
conference is genuinely international in participation and the acceptance rate
is competitive, but at 180 attendees it is a specialist venue rather than a
major one. Attorney should decide how to characterize this.
