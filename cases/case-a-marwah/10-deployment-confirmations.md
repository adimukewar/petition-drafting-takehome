## Cascadia Regional Agricultural Board

**From:** operations@cascadia-rab.example.gov
**Date:** 8 May 2026
**Subject:** Confirmation of Lyra deployment — for immigration filing

To whom it may concern,

This confirms that the Cascadia Regional Agricultural Board has operated the
Lyra segmentation method in production since the 2024 growing season.

Scope of use:
- All seasonal UAV survey imagery (approximately 40,000 acres/season)
- Output drives inspector dispatch prioritization
- Runs on Board infrastructure; no vendor contract

The Board did not pay a license fee. The method was implemented from the
published description with support from Dr. Marwah's group at Verdant Systems.

Marguerite Adeyemi-Stroud
Director of Field Operations

## Sonoran Valley Water and Crop Authority

**From:** t.reyes-abbot@svwca.example.gov
**Date:** 10 May 2026
**Subject:** RE: Lyra — deployment confirmation request

Confirming for your filing:

SVWCA adopted the Lyra method for the 2025 season across our tree-nut monitoring
program (approx. 11,000 acres). We evaluated it against our previous
vegetation-index screening and against one commercial product.

We selected it primarily because it retrained on our crop mix with about 200
annotated points, where the commercial option required a labeled dataset we
would have had to commission.

I should be accurate: we have one season of operational data, so I would not yet
claim a durable performance figure. What I can say is that we adopted it, we are
continuing with it for 2026, and it is now our primary screening method.

Teodoro Reyes-Abbot
Deputy Director, Crop Health Programs
