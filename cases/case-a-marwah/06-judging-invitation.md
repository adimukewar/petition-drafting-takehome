**From:** program@nurs.example.org

**Date:** 17 January 2024
**Subject:** Invitation to judge — NURS 2024, Agricultural Technology track

Dear Dr. Marwah,

The Nationwide Undergraduate Research Symposium invites you to serve as a judge
in the Agricultural Technology track for our 2024 program.

NURS receives roughly 1,900 submissions annually across all tracks from
undergraduate researchers at accredited institutions nationwide. The
Agricultural Technology track received 84 submissions last year. Judges evaluate
written submissions and, for finalists, live presentations.

We select judges who are active researchers in the relevant area. Your work on
agricultural vision was recommended to us by Prof. Adaeze Oyelaran.

The commitment is approximately eight hours across the judging period.

Sincerely,
Renata Okonkwo-Bayliss
Program Director, NURS
