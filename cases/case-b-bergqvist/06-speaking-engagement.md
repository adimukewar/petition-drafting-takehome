# Speaking Engagement — DistSysDays 2024

## Event description (from event website)

> **DistSysDays 2024**
> Austin, TX · 12 October 2024
>
> A one-day community gathering for people who work on distributed systems.
> Organized by volunteers, hosted at the Wrenfield Coworking Commons. Free to
> attend, capacity 120.
>
> No formal review process — talks are selected by the organizing committee from
> community submissions. We aim for a mix of experience levels and we especially
> encourage first-time speakers.
