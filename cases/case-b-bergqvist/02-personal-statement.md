# Personal Statement — Tomas Bergqvist

I have spent my career on a problem most people never see: how do you decide
which machine runs which piece of work, when the machines are not the same as
each other and the work is not the same as itself?

Ferrymark is my answer. I started it in 2020 because every scheduler I had used
assumed a homogeneous fleet, and no real fleet is homogeneous. You have old
machines and new ones, GPU nodes and CPU nodes, spot capacity that vanishes.
Existing schedulers treated that as a configuration problem. I treated it as the
central problem.

Ferrymark now has over 4,200 stars on GitHub and more than 90 contributors. It
is used in production by companies including Ironwood Freight, Bellhaven
Analytics, and Quietwater Media. I did not market it. People found it because it
solved something.

At Halcyon Grid I lead an engineering organization of more than fifty people
across platform, infrastructure, and reliability. I set technical direction for
our scheduling platform, which processes several billion task executions
annually. My work there has been recognized internally with the company's
Innovation Award.

I hold a patent for adaptive work distribution in heterogeneous clusters — the
core idea underlying Ferrymark's placement algorithm. I was invited to keynote
DistSysDays in 2024, where I presented this work to the distributed systems
community.

I believe my contributions have shaped how the industry thinks about scheduling
in heterogeneous environments. The ideas I introduced in Ferrymark have been
adopted by other projects and are becoming standard practice. When I look at
scheduler designs published in the last two years, I see the influence of the
approach I pioneered.

The United States is where this work belongs. The concentration of
infrastructure companies here, the density of engineers who care about these
problems, and the open-source culture that made Ferrymark possible in the first
place are not replicated anywhere else. I want to keep building here.

*Tomas Bergqvist*
*19 May 2026*
