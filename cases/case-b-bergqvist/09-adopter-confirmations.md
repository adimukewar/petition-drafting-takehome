## Ironwood Freight

**From:** s.marchetti-obi@ironwoodfreight.example.com

**Subject:** RE: Ferrymark usage confirmation

Hi Tomas,

Happy to confirm. We've run Ferrymark in production since early 2023 — it
schedules our entire route-optimization batch tier, roughly 400,000 jobs a day
across about 1,200 mixed-class nodes.

We moved to it specifically because our fleet is a mess of three hardware
generations plus spot capacity, and our previous scheduler treated all of that
as equivalent. Ferrymark's placement model was the only thing we evaluated that
handled it natively.

For whatever it's worth in your filing: we evaluated four options and this was
the one we picked on the merits. We have no commercial relationship with you and
have never paid anything for it.
